# User-experience pass, second round, 2026-11-26

Asked for by Mikey after #166 to #173 merged: "a full round including the
ones that you skipped last time and also in this payment ... we will use
PayPal on hq". Done on staging plus the card payments branch (#174), as an
admin and as a client, on a laptop and a 390-pixel phone.

Like the first pass it was a script driving a real browser, with every
screen's words read back, script and server errors watched for, and
accessibility and sideways overflow checked on each page. A person using HQ
for a day will still find things this didn't.

## Walked

- **Every staff page**: all 73 addresses in the sidebar and search, plus a
  client, project, opportunity, proposal and four invoices (sent, overdue,
  paid, draft), each on a laptop and a phone.
- **The billing pages by hand, on a phone** (#166, #167, #173): a one-off
  charge (empty, a bad amount, sent), a plan saved, paused and started, prepaid
  hours with their invoice, a renewal billed and repriced, a promise to pay,
  a payment that is too much, one with a card number as its reference, a part
  payment, a write-off, Money, Invoices and its filters, the cash forecast,
  the renewals year, and how clients pay.
- **Every portal page**, phone and laptop, and the four skipped last time:
  the website editor (a notice, sending changes, coming soon), social posts
  (asking for a change, approving a month), a monthly report, and a call
  invite (marking times, saving).
- **Paying by card with PayPal**: PayPal's API and approval page were
  stood in for, shaped as its documentation gives them. Backing out,
  paying, coming back with PayPal's own additions on the address, reloading,
  the webhook alone, and what staff then see.

## Bugs fixed

- **A phone's top bar while a call is open** ran 160 pixels off the side of
  every page, because "On a call with <client>" didn't wrap. It wraps now.
- **A proposal's page on a phone** ran off the side: the estimate table
  pushed the whole form wide. Forms no longer grow past the screen.
- **"(optional) (optional)"** on Due on, Last one by, Price, Note and What
  they said.
- **A renewal with no cost entered counted as costing nothing** on the
  renewals year, so the year read as earning more than it does. The page now
  says how many have no cost, and their cell says "Not set".
- **A bank account number was let into "How clients pay"**, which every
  client reads: only card-length numbers were refused. An account or routing
  number is refused now; a phone number or PO box still passes.
- **Ask HQ told an admin "this account has not been given access"** when the
  feature was only off. It says it is off and where to turn it on.
- **Backing out at the payment platform** came back to the portal with no
  word and the platform's token on the address. It says nothing was charged.
- **An invoice voided, or paid by check, while the client was at PayPal**
  would have been charged when they came back. PayPal is not asked for the
  money unless the invoice is still open and owes what it did.

## Made consistent

- Invoice dates read "Mon, Nov 23" on an invoice, the Invoices list, a
  project's Money and prepaid hours, as they do everywhere else.
- A one-off invoice reads "For: A one-off charge", not the form's
  "Something else".
- A client's invoice list names what each is for, as Money → Invoices does,
  where it said "No project" for every one.
- A payment, by hand or by card, shows in its invoice's "What changed here".
- A draft invoice no longer says the client "already sees" Pay by card.
- "Overdue $42.50 Overdue" on a client's Money is one word.
- "A 30 minutes call" is "a call ... 30 minutes long".
- The Instagram and LinkedIn page hints no longer give a Facebook example.

## Left as it is

- **This machine shows some dates oddly** ("November 1, 2026" for a month,
  a year on dates in this year). It runs an older Node than HQ does and
  words dates differently; the tests that pin the right wording fail here
  for the same reason and pass in CI. Not checked on the real version.
- **PayPal itself was not used.** Its calls follow its documentation and
  were run against a stand-in. A sandbox payment on staging is still the
  first real proof (docs/operations.md, "Card payments").
- Money's tables for time and expenses still show days as 2026-11-23.
- A client's activity line for a change they made to their site reads
  "Changed whether a website is open", with no "A client".
- "Pay invoice INV-0001" at the top of the portal still says "How to pay"
  and goes to the invoices, where Pay by card is.
- The things left in the first pass (docs/reviews/2026-11-25-ux-pass.md)
  are unchanged.

## Checks

Type check and the three lints pass. Unit, integration and browser suites
fail only where staging already fails on this machine.
