# User-experience pass, 2026-11-25

Every feature of decisions 0015 and 0016 (#169 to #171), and where they
meet #166 to #168 and #173 on `pre-staging`, walked end to end in a real
browser: an admin, a member, an admin viewing as a member, and a client in
the portal, on a laptop and a 390-pixel phone. Each screen's words were
read, script and server errors watched for, and accessibility and sideways
overflow checked. This page records what was found and what was done.

It was automated: a script drove the browser and the screens and their
text were read back. A person using HQ for a day will still find things
this didn't.

## Walked

Home and Get HQ ready; the eight hubs, pins and Recent; the command bar
(empty, typed, no match, moves, quick add, search, shortcuts); the
assistant on Home and in the drawer (an answer, a lookup, cards, Do it,
Cancel, Undo, small changes going straight through, a failed call, New
chat); Undo and Recent changes, with a repeating task; saved replies and
the picker; duplicate clients and merging; notice settings; Settings and
phone alerts; System status; Team workload; All work and many at once;
People and View as; the calendar feed; "What changed here"; the portal's
first page, a project's timeline, the sign-in link and approving from an
email's link; every page in the sidebar on a phone.

## Bugs fixed

- **Phone: Undo was under the tab bar.** #168's Undo bar and #170's tab bar
  both sat at the bottom; the bar was hidden and wider than the screen.
- **Merging left notes behind.** Notes point at a client by kind and id,
  not a foreign key, so the merge didn't find them.
- **Saved replies didn't fill `{client}` or `{first name}`** on a project.
  They are the client's name and its main (or only) contact's first name.
  A placeholder HQ can't fill is named under the box.
- **Enter in the command bar could move a deal.** With a deal's name typed,
  the move was the first pick. Pages and records come first now.
- **A message Claude didn't answer** stayed in the conversation with nothing
  under it. The conversation now says so.
- **A change the assistant couldn't set up** beside one it could was
  dropped without a word. It is said.
- **Unsent text** was kept after a send that came back with a page of its
  own, and dropped after signing in again following a lost send.
- **Pages nobody could find.** Refused attempts, and on `pre-staging`
  Money → Invoices, Cash forecast and the renewals year, were not in the
  sidebar or search. A test now fails when a staff page is missing.

## Made consistent

- A small change done from an assistant card, and a batch on All work, can
  be undone from the Undo bar, Ctrl+Z and Recent changes like any other.
- Merging asks first. The client merged away links to the one kept, can't
  be merged again and isn't offered as a look-alike.
- The duplicate question is beside Add client, where the page comes back.
- An assistant card isn't repeated in a bubble; Home's cards lost a second
  border; New chat and "Do the first one" show only when they mean
  something.
- Saved replies, the picker and the merge form use HQ's own fields.
- System status says Fix only where something is wrong. The calendar feed
  says it carries booked calls. "Calendar hours" is "Hours for calls", as
  its page is.
- The portal offers a sign-in link only when HQ can send email. A step
  that is the client's goes to "Send us a file".
- Get HQ ready stacks on a phone.

## Left as it is

- The assistant shows `**bold**` and `- lists` as typed. Its instructions
  ask for plain sentences; a real model mostly complies.
- Labels added to many tasks at once have no Undo; they come off by hand.
- With email not set up, "Fix" on System status and Get HQ ready point at
  the operations article, which exists only once the knowledge base has
  been filled from `docs/`.
- Phone alerts couldn't be exercised: the test browser blocks them.
