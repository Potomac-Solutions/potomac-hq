# User-experience pass on the eleven extras, 2026-11-26

Asked for by Mikey once #176 merged into staging: "let's run another
usability audit". Done on staging as it stood, as a client on a 390-pixel
phone and a laptop, and as an admin on both.

As before it was a script driving a real browser: each new screen used the
way a person would (typing, tapping, getting it wrong first), its words read
back, script and server errors watched for, and accessibility and sideways
overflow checked on every page. The payment platform and the text sender
were stood in for. A person using it for a week will still find things this
didn't.

## Walked

- **As a client, on a phone:** the portal's first page with every new panel;
  turning texts on (a bad number first) and off; setting up automatic
  payment, changing the account and backing out; the request box (empty, then
  sent, then declined by staff); the intake form (a password typed into it,
  then sent properly); rating a finished project low; accepting a proposal
  and paying its deposit straight away; and what the first page says after a
  plan bill's automatic payment is declined.
- **As staff, laptop and phone:** a client's page with requests, intake
  answers, automatic payment and the handoff pack (downloaded); a project's
  rating; Today with a call to make; Money with late fees and the tax pack;
  a late invoice and its fee; profit by client; and a refund (too much, then
  part of a payment), with what the client then sees.

Nothing overflowed sideways, no script or server errors, and no
accessibility findings on any of these screens.

## Bugs fixed

- **The intake form lost everything typed when it was refused.** Type a
  password into one box of a long form and every other answer was gone. Now
  the form comes back as typed, with only the password-like answer emptied,
  and says it isn't saved yet. The password is kept nowhere.
- **A refund billed the client again unless a box was ticked.** Giving $20
  back on a paid invoice put the invoice back to "waiting for payment", and
  "Pay invoice … $20.00" on the client's first page. The form now asks "Do
  they owe it again?", and the answer already picked is no; "yes" is for a
  payment that didn't stand (a bounced check, a dispute). A reason is asked
  for either way.
- **Turning late fees on charged invoices that were already late.** Setting a
  fee of "10 days late" put a fee, the next morning, on an invoice 35 days
  late under the old no-fee terms. A fee now applies only to invoices that
  reach their day after it was set, and the Money page says so.
- **A client on automatic payment was told to pay their plan bill**, as
  "waiting on you", for the hours before the morning run; and **when the
  charge was declined they were told nothing**, while their panel still said
  bills are paid automatically. A plan bill that will be paid automatically
  now says so and isn't listed as waiting; a declined one says so, with the
  day of the next try and what they can do; after three tries it says it is
  theirs to pay. Staff see the same on the client's page, in words rather
  than PayPal's code alone.
- **A rating's note was lost** when the number was tapped first, since a
  number sends the form. The note now comes first, and the numbers say that
  tapping one sends it.
- **A client who had just rated a project 2 of 5 was asked for a public
  review** in the next panel. The review isn't asked for while a rating is
  waiting, or after a low one.

## Made clearer

- **The portal's first page**: proposals, invoices and automatic payment now
  sit together, above the settings-like panels; "About your business" is near
  the top until it's sent, then drops to the bottom beside text reminders.
  Before, four new panels sat between a client and their invoices.
- A request about a project that has since finished names the project,
  instead of "A finished project".
- Intake answers on the client's page read in Potomac's words ("Where the
  domain is registered"), not the client's form's ("your domain").
- Profit by client has a total for Invoiced, and says "None logged" and "Not
  known" where it left hours and per-hour cells empty.
- Automatic payment says so if it can't start, instead of returning to the
  portal with no word.
- The late fee's amount says to leave it empty for None.

## Left as it is, on purpose

- **"Written off" on an invoice after an ordinary refund.** A refund the
  client doesn't owe again closes the invoice the way a write-off does, so it
  reads "Written off" and shows in the tax pack's written-off list as well as
  under refunds. A status of its own ("Refunded") would read better; that is
  a change to the invoice model, not to a screen.
- **A form that is refused comes back empty** elsewhere (a bad mobile number
  for texts, a refund of too much). Only the intake is long enough for that
  to cost a person real work.
- **The client's page in HQ is very long** on a phone (about seventeen
  screens with everything open). It was long before these features; it wants
  a pass of its own.
- **A monthly plan bill is named with a full date** ("Hosting and upkeep,
  November 26, 2026") on this machine only: its older Node formats dates
  differently, which is also why a known set of tests fails here and not in
  CI.

## Not proven

As in decision 0019: no real PayPal account has saved a method or been
charged, and no real text has gone through Twilio. This pass used stand-ins
for both.
